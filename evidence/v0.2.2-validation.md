short inter-activation absent intervals do not resolve longer absent-target
reliability. No detector tuning was included in this diagnostic patch.

recovery-counter zeroing was not independently verified from a supplied
counter summary. Source inspection shows the intended counter assignments
and preservation of timer/tracking/session fields.

Initial pre-icon estimate clearing was not directly exercised in the
supplied logs. A fresh-sprint estimate clearing is a different branch.

Switching away from the game clears the session through the existing
focus guard. Clicking Clear after switching away therefore cannot prove
uninterrupted active timer or track retention.

No guaranteed capture latency, universal false-positive reliability,
cross-resolution reliability, or anti-cheat approval is established.
Raw logs and recordings remain local and are not included in this note.
